## NISAB VALUES (Minimum Threshold for Zakat)

### Physical Standards (Permanent — Never Change)
| Standard | Weight | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Gold Nisab | **87.48 grams** (7.5 tola) | Prophet ﷺ established |
| Silver Nisab | **612.36 grams** (52.5 tola) | Prophet ﷺ established |

### PKR Equivalent (May 2026 — UPDATE MONTHLY)
| Standard | Weight | Rate/gram | PKR Value |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gold Nisab | 87.48g | ~Rs. 18,000/g* | ~Rs. **1,574,640** |
| Silver Nisab | 612.36g | ~Rs. 210/g* | ~Rs. **128,596** |

*Verify current rates at: bullionrates.pk or sbp.org.pk

### Which Nisab to Use?
```
For ZAKAT ON GOLD:    Use Gold Nisab (87.48g)
For ZAKAT ON SILVER:  Use Silver Nisab (612.36g)
For ZAKAT ON CASH:    Silver Nisab (Rs. ~128,000) — most scholars' view
For ZAKAT ON STOCKS:  Silver Nisab — more conservative, benefits more people
For ZAKAT ON BUSINESS: Silver Nisab
```

**General Rule:** Silver nisab is lower, so more people pay Zakat.
Most Pakistani scholars and the majority use silver nisab for cash and assets.

## GOLD JEWELRY — SPECIAL RULING

**Scholarly Disagreement:**

**Opinion 1 (Majority — Hanafi, Maliki, Hanbali):**
Zakat IS payable on gold jewelry above nisab
Rate: 2.5% on current market value

**Opinion 2 (Shafi'i — minority):**
Zakat NOT payable on gold jewelry worn regularly for personal use

**Practical Guidance for Pakistan (Hanafi majority):**
```
Gold jewelry > 87.48 grams → Zakat payable
Zakat = (Total gold grams × current gold rate per gram) × 2.5%

Example:
  Total gold jewelry: 150 grams
  Current 24K rate:   Rs. 18,000/gram
  Total value:        Rs. 2,700,000
  Zakat:              Rs. 2,700,000 × 2.5% = Rs. 67,500

Note: If jewelry is less than 87.48g but combined with cash
      pushes you above silver nisab, zakat still applies.
```

## LUNAR CALENDAR REFERENCE

Zakat is based on the **Islamic lunar year (Hawl)** — not solar year.

```
Lunar year = 354 days (≈ 11 days shorter than solar year)
1 solar year = 1.0307 lunar years

Practical approach: Most Pakistani Muslims pay Zakat in Ramadan.
This is acceptable — you may pay early (before the exact hawl date).
You should NOT pay late (after hawl date without reason).
```

**Ramadan Zakat:** Paying in Ramadan is highly recommended as it multiplies reward,
but is not strictly required for validity.

## ASSETS EXEMPT FROM ZAKAT

```
❌ Primary home (one home you live in)
❌ Personal vehicle (for own transportation)
❌ Personal clothing and household items
❌ Tools of trade (used for work, not for sale)
❌ Accounts receivable that are doubtful/bad debts
❌ Business assets NOT held for sale (machines, equipment)
```

## WHO RECEIVES ZAKAT (8 Categories — Quran 9:60)

```
1. Al-Fuqara (الفقراء)    — The poor (have some but below nisab)
2. Al-Masakeen (المساكين) — The destitute (have nothing)
3. Al-Amileen (العاملين)  — Zakat administrators/collectors
4. Al-Mu'allafat          — New Muslims / hearts to be reconciled
5. Fil-Riqab (الرقاب)     — Freeing slaves (historical — not applicable today)
6. Al-Gharimeen (الغارمين)— Those in debt (not from sin)
7. Fi Sabilillah (في سبيل الله) — In the path of Allah (scholars disagree on scope)
8. Ibn al-Sabeel (ابن السبيل) — Stranded travelers
```

**In Pakistan context — Priority recipients:**
1. Genuinely poor families (Fuqara, Masakeen)
2. People with crushing debt (Gharimeen)
3. Students seeking Islamic education (Fi Sabilillah — common view)
4. Stranded travelers / refugees
