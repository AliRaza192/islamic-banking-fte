Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 5, 2026

1. What We Collect

Simple answer: We only collect what's needed to provide you the service. We do NOT sell your data.
  • Email address — Only if you create an account (for OTP login)
  • Chat messages — Your questions and our responses (to provide the service and improve quality)
  • Usage data — IP address, browser type (for rate limiting and security)
  • Payment info — Processed by Stripe (we never see your card number)

2. What We DON'T Collect

  • We do NOT collect your CNIC, bank account numbers, or other national ID numbers
  • We do NOT track your location
  • We do NOT share your data with third parties for advertising
  • We do NOT sell your personal information to anyone

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide Islamic banking guidance and calculations
  • To remember your conversation context within a session
  • To enforce rate limits and prevent abuse
  • To improve our AI's accuracy (anonymized data only)
  • To send payment confirmations (if you subscribe)

4. Data Storage & Security

  • Data is stored in Neon PostgreSQL (encrypted at rest)
  • All communication is over HTTPS
  • We use industry-standard security practices
  • Financial data you share in chat is NOT stored separately — it exists only in your chat messages

5. Data Retention

  • Chat messages: Retained for 12 months, then automatically deleted
  • Account data: Retained until you delete your account
  • Payment records: Retained as required by law (5 years for tax purposes)

6. Your Rights

  • Access: You can request a copy of all data we have about you
  • Deletion: You can request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Correction: You can request correction of any inaccurate data
  • Export: You can request your data in a portable format

7. Cookies

We use only essential cookies (for authentication). We do NOT use tracking cookies or advertising cookies.

8. Children's Privacy

This service is not intended for children under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Significant changes will be announced on our website. Continued use of the service constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact Us

For privacy-related questions or data deletion requests, contact us at: privacy@islamic-banking-fte.vercel.app
