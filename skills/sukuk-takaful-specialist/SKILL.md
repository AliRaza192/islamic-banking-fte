## AAOIFI Shariah Standards Reference

### Sukuk (AAOIFI Shariah Standard 17)
- Sukuk are certificates of equal value representing ownership in tangible assets
- NOT debt instruments — holders own proportional share of underlying asset
- Returns come from rental/profit of underlying assets, NOT from interest
- SPV (Special Purpose Vehicle) holds assets on behalf of sukuk holders

### Sukuk Classification
- Sukuk Al-Ijara: Asset-backed, rental income
- Sukuk Al-Wakala: Agency-based, profit from managed portfolio
- Sukuk Al-Mudarabah: Profit-sharing partnership
- Sukuk Al-Musharaka: Joint venture partnership

### DUAL-REGIME ACCOUNTING (Sukuk)

**Investor Side — AAOIFI FAS 25:**
- Held to Maturity → Amortised cost
- Trading → FVTPL
- Available for Sale → FVOCI

**Investor Side — IFRS 9:**
- Two-step: Business Model test + SPPI test
- Ijara Sukuk (fixed rental): SPPI PASS → Amortised cost or FVOCI
- Musharakah Sukuk (profit-contingent): SPPI FAIL → FVTPL
- ECL staging for impairment (Stage 1/2/3)

**Issuer Side — AAOIFI FAS 33:**
- Failed derecognition (most common): Asset stays on books, Sukuk = liability
- True derecognition (rare): Asset removed, gain/loss recognized

**Issuer Side — IFRS 9/32:**
- Same derecognition test as AAOIFI
- Purchase undertaking at face value = failed derecognition
- Sukuk proceeds classified as Financial Liability

### DUAL-REGIME ACCOUNTING (Takaful)

**Operator Accounting — IFRS 17:**
- GMM (General Measurement Model) for long-term family Takaful
- VFA (Variable Fee Approach) for direct-participation contracts
- PAA (Premium Allocation Approach) for short-term general Takaful (≤12 months)
- CSM (Contractual Service Margin) = unearned profit

**Operator Accounting — AAOIFI FAS 12:**
- Tabarru (donation) income recognition
- Wakala fee as operator's income
- Surplus belongs to participants (not operator)
- Qard Hasan for deficit (interest-free loan from operator)

### Income Labels (Sukuk)
- Pakistan: "Profit Distribution to Sukuk Holders"
- UAE: "Profit Distribution to Sukuk Holders"
- Saudi Arabia: "Profit Distribution to Sukuk Holders"
- Bahrain: "Profit Distributed to Sukuk Holders"
- NEVER: "Coupon Payment" or "Interest Payment"

### Takaful (AAOIFI Shariah Standard 26)
- Takaful is mutual insurance — participants contribute to shared risk pool
- NOT conventional insurance — no gharar, maysir, or riba
- Operator (wakeel) manages pool for fee (wakala model)
- Surplus belongs to participants, not the company
- Deficit covered by Qard Hasan (interest-free loan) from shareholders

## SUKUK SECTION

### What Makes Sukuk Halal
- Backed by REAL assets (not just debt)
- Holders own a share of the ASSET, not a loan receivable
- Returns come from asset rental/profit, not interest

### Pakistan GOP Ijara Sukuk
- Government sells assets to Sukuk holders, leases them back
- Rental payments = Sukuk "profit" payments
- Available through: All Islamic banks, PSX
- Minimum: Usually Rs. 100,000 retail

### Sukuk Yield Calculation
```
Current Yield = (Annual Rental Payment ÷ Current Market Price) × 100

Example:
  Face value: Rs. 100,000 | Annual rental: Rs. 16,000 | Market price: Rs. 99,000
  Yield = (16,000 ÷ 99,000) × 100 = 16.16%
```

## TAKAFUL SECTION

### Why Conventional Insurance is Problematic
- Gharar: Outcome uncertain (you may pay for years and get nothing)
- Maysir: Element of chance
- Riba: Insurance companies invest premiums in interest-bearing instruments

### How Takaful Works (Wakala Model — Used in Pakistan)
```
You → Pay contribution → Split into:
  1. Tabarru (donation to risk pool) → pays claims
  2. Wakala fee (operator's management charge, ~20-30%)

If pool has surplus → returned to participants (NOT kept by company)
If pool has deficit → participants may contribute more OR company covers via Qard Hasan
```

### Pakistani Takaful Operators
| Company | Type | Products |
|---|---|---|
| Pak-Qatar Family Takaful | Dedicated Takaful | Life, savings, health |
| Pak-Qatar General Takaful | Dedicated Takaful | Property, vehicle, fire |
| EFU Life (Takaful window) | Window | Life Takaful |
| Jubilee Life (Takaful window) | Window | Life Takaful |
| TPL Trakker | Vehicle Takaful | Car/bike Takaful |

### Vehicle Takaful Example
```
Car value: Rs. 3,000,000
Annual contribution: Rs. 90,000
  → Tabarru (risk pool): Rs. 67,500
  → Wakala fee: Rs. 22,500
Claim process: Same as conventional insurance
```
