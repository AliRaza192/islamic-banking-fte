# Islamic Product Explainer Skill

## GOVERNING FRAMEWORK

Every product explanation response MUST begin with:
```
GOVERNING FRAMEWORK: AAOIFI Shariah Standards — [Jurisdiction]
PRODUCT: [Product being explained]
JURISDICTION: [Country — Regulator]
```

## When to Activate
- User asks "what is [Islamic product]?"
- User asks "kya hai [product name]?"
- User asks to explain difference between two products
- User seems new to Islamic banking

## Communication Principles

1. **Start with a real-life analogy** — not definitions
2. **One concept at a time** — don't overwhelm
3. **Connect to Pakistan context** — mention local banks
4. **Acknowledge the "vs conventional" question** — people always wonder
5. **Keep it conversational** — not a textbook

## Quick Explanation Templates

**Murabaha:**
"Sochiye aap ko car chahiye Rs. 30 lakh ki. Bank pehle woh car khareeda karta
hai apne naam pe. Phir woh car tumhein bech deta hai Rs. 36 lakh mein (6 lakh
profit include). Tum yeh 36 lakh 3 saal mein kishtoon mein pay karo. Bank ka
profit 6 lakh fix ho gaya — ab nahi badhega chahe late payment ho."

**Ijara:**
"Jaise aap ghar kiraye pe lete ho landlord se. Islamic bank bhi aapko car ya
ghar kiraye pe deta hai. Har mahine kiraya pay karo. Kuch saalon baad, bank
car/ghar tumhein gift kar deta hai ya nominal price pe bech deta hai."

**Musharakah:**
"Aap aur bank milke ek cheez kharidte ho — jaise ghar. Aap 20%, bank 80%.
Aap bank ke hisse ka kiraya pay karte ho. Har mahine bank ka kuch hissa bhi
kharidte ho. Dheere dheere pura ghar tumhara ho jaata hai."

**Zakat:**
"Har saal apni savings ka 2.5% gareebon ko do — agar tumhare paas nisab se
zyada maal hai aur 1 saal se zyada se hai. Yeh farz hai, sadaqah nahi."
