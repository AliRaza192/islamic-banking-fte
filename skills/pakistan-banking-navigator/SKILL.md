## Key Pakistan-Specific Knowledge

### SBP Islamic Banking Framework
- **Regulator:** State Bank of Pakistan, Islamic Banking Department (IBD)
- **Website:** sbp.org.pk/ibd/index.asp
- **Key Document:** Instructions for Islamic Banking (IIB) — consolidated circular
- **Shariah Governance:** Each bank must have SBP-approved Shariah Supervisory Board
- **Reporting:** Islamic Banking Bulletin published quarterly by SBP

### KIBOR Reference Rates (Check sbp.org.pk for current)
```
Most home/car Ijara rates = KIBOR + Spread
Typical spread: 1.5% to 3% above KIBOR
KIBOR itself changes with SBP policy rate decisions
```

### Automatic Zakat Deduction (Pakistan-Specific)
```
Who:   All Muslims with savings accounts in Pakistani banks
When:  1st Ramadan every year
Rate:  2.5% on balance (if above nisab)
Where: Goes to Central Zakat Fund (distributed to poor)
Opt out: CZ-50 form before 1 Sha'ban to your bank branch
```

### Roshan Digital Account (Overseas Pakistanis)
All major banks offer Islamic Roshan Digital Accounts:
- Meezan Bank Roshan Islamic Account
- HBL Roshan Islamic Account
- Bank Islami Roshan Account
- Dubai Islamic Bank Roshan Account

Features: Account opening from abroad, foreign currency or PKR,
Naya Pakistan Islamic Certificate (Shariah-compliant — verify current status)

### Complaint Process (Pakistan)
1. First: Complain to your bank's Shariah department
2. Then: SBP Banking Mohtasib (Ombudsman) — bankingmohtasib.gov.pk
3. For Shariah issues: SBP Islamic Banking Department

## KIBOR RATES — How to Use

KIBOR (Karachi Interbank Offered Rate) Pakistani Islamic banks ka benchmark hai.

**Current Range (2026):** 18-21% per annum (verify: sbp.org.pk)

**Home Finance Formula:**
`Profit Rate = KIBOR (6-month) + Bank Spread (1.5% to 3%)`

Example: KIBOR 19% + Meezan 2.5% spread = **21.5% annual**

**User ko batao:** "KIBOR monthly change hoti hai — sbp.org.pk pe current rate check karein."
