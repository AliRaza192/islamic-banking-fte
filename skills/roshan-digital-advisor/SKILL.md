## NAYA PAKISTAN CERTIFICATE (NPC)

**Structure:** Ijarah Sukuk — Shariah compliant  
**Tenures:** 3 months, 6 months, 1 year, 3 years, 5 years  
**Currency:** PKR aur USD dono  
**Min:** USD $500 / PKR 50,000  
**Tax:** 15% WHT filers, 30% non-filers

---

## HOW TO OPEN RDA

1. Bank website → "Roshan Digital Account"
2. Documents: CNIC/NICOP, overseas proof, income proof
3. Online form + video KYC
4. Initial deposit (wire transfer)
5. Active in 3-7 working days

## COMMON QUESTIONS

**Q: Kya RDA se Pakistan mein ghar khareed sakte hain?**  
A: Haan — Meezan Easy Home, DIB Al Islami Home Finance RDA se link kar sakte hain.

**Q: Kya profit halal hai?**  
A: Islamic banks ke RDA Mudarabah model pe hain — profit share, interest nahi.

**Q: Tax?**  
A: NPC profit pe WHT lagta hai. Tax advisor se confirm karein.
