## Key IFRS Divergences

### IAH Classification (Most Significant)
- **AAOIFI:** Investment Account Holders' funds = "Equity of IAH" (not a liability)
- **IFRS:** IAH funds = "Financial Liabilities" under IAS 32
- **Impact:** Affects balance sheet size, capital ratios, leverage calculations

### Ijara (Lease) Accounting
- **AAOIFI FAS 32:** Asset stays on lessor's balance sheet, depreciate over useful life
- **IFRS 16:** May derecognize if finance lease (5 indicators test)
- **Impact:** Balance sheet presentation, depreciation expense timing

### Income Recognition (Murabaha)
- **AAOIFI FAS 2:** Deferred Murabaha Income recognized over contract period
- **IFRS 9:** Effective interest rate (EIR) method — front-loaded recognition
- **Impact:** Profit recognition pattern differs (flat vs declining balance)

### Sukuk Issuer (Derecognition)
- **AAOIFI FAS 33:** Failed derecognition = asset stays on books, sukuk = liability
- **IFRS 9/32:** Same test, but AAOIFI more prescriptive about purchase undertaking
- **Impact:** Most ijarah sukuk fail derecognition under both standards

### Zakat
- **AAOIFI FAS 9:** Detailed zakatable asset classification and formula
- **IFRS:** No equivalent standard — zakat is an Islamic-specific obligation
- **Impact:** AAOIFI provides more comprehensive guidance
