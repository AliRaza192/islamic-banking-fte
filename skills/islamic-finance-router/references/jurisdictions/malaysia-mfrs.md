# Malaysia — Islamic Banking Jurisdiction Overlay

## Regulator
Bank Negara Malaysia (BNM)

## Accounting Framework
MFRS (Malaysian Financial Reporting Standards) — equivalent to IFRS

## Key Regulations
- Islamic Financial Services Act 2013 (IFSA)
- BNM Policy Documents on Islamic Banking
- Shariah Advisory Council (SAC) rulings

## Currency
MYR (Malaysian Ringgit)

## Number Formatting
Standard international: thousands, millions, billions

## Major Islamic Banks
1. Maybank Islamic (largest Islamic bank in ASEAN)
2. CIMB Islamic Bank
3. Bank Islam Malaysia
4. RHB Islamic Bank
5. Hong Leong Islamic Bank
6. Affin Islamic Bank

## Profit Rate Benchricks
- KLIBOR (Kuala Lumpur Interbank Offered Rate)
- BNM uses BNM-RSRR (Reference Rate for Shariah-compliant financing)
- Banks use BNM-RSRR + spread for pricing

## Zakat Treatment
- Voluntary for Islamic financial institutions
- AAOIFI GS9 calculation method (Hanafi or Shafi'i)
- Annual disclosure of zakat paid (if any)
- Individual zakat collected by state zakat authorities

## Key Differences from Reference Standard
- BNM has comprehensive Islamic banking framework
- Malaysia uses MFRS (equivalent to IFRS)
- Shariah Advisory Council (SAC) is the supreme authority
- Dual banking system (conventional + Islamic windows)
- Malaysia leads in Islamic capital markets (sukuk)
- BNM-RSRR is Shariah-compliant benchmark (unlike KIBOR in Pakistan)

## Income Labels (Malaysia)
- Murabaha: "Profit from Islamic Financing" or "Islamic Financing Income"
- Ijara: "Rental Income from Ijara"
- Musharakah: "Profit from Musharakah Financing"
- Sukuk: "Profit Distribution to Sukuk Holders"
- NEVER: "Interest Income" or "Coupon Payment"

## Balance Sheet Classification
- Islamic financing receivables shown separately
- "Islamic Financing" as separate balance sheet category
- Deferred Murabaha Income netted for amortised cost presentation

## Disclosure Requirements
- BNM quarterly regulatory returns
- SAC rulings compliance statement
- Shariah Advisory Council report in annual report
- Segment reporting for Islamic banking activities
- AAOIFI-style disclosures common
