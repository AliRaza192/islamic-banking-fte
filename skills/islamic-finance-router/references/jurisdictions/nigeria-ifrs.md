# Nigeria — Islamic Banking Jurisdiction Overlay

## Regulator
Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN) — Non-Interest Banking Framework

## Accounting Framework
IFRS as adopted by Nigeria (IFRS 9, IFRS 16, etc.) with CBN Non-Interest Banking Guidelines

## Key Regulations
- CBN Non-Interest Banking Framework (2011)
- CBN Guidelines on Islamic Banking (revised)
- Financial Reporting Council of Nigeria (FRCN) — IFRS adoption
- Nigeria Deposit Insurance Corporation (NDIC) — deposit protection
- AAOIFI standards referenced but not mandatory

## Currency
NGN (Nigerian Naira)

## Number Formatting
Standard international formatting (millions/billions)

## Major Islamic/Non-Interest Banks
1. Jaiz Bank Plc — first full-fledged Islamic bank
2. Taj Bank Limited
3. Lotus Bank Limited
4. Stanbic IBTC (non-interest banking window)
5. Sterling Bank (non-interest banking window)

## Profit Rate Benchmarks
- NIBOR (Nigerian Interbank Offered Rate) — conventional benchmark
- CBN Monetary Policy Rate (MPR) as reference
- Islamic banks use NIBOR + spread, labeled as "profit rate"

## Zakat Treatment
- No formal government zakat collection
- Voluntary individual obligation
- Zakat organizations: Jaiz Bank Zakat Committee, NASFAT Zakat
- Corporate zakat: discretionary, no tax deduction

## Key Differences from Reference Standard
- Non-Interest Banking Framework applies to both Islamic and other non-interest models
- Nigeria has growing but still small Islamic finance sector
- CBN requires separate license for non-interest banking
- AAOIFI standards are reference only
- Sovereign Sukuk programme since 2017 (DMO Sukuk)
- Islamic finance growing in Northern Nigeria (largely Muslim population)

## Income Labels (Nigeria)
- Murabaha: "Profit from Murabaha Financing"
- Ijara: "Rental Income from Ijara"
- Mudarabah: "Profit Share from Mudarabah"
- Musharakah: "Profit from Musharakah"
- NEVER: "Interest Income" or "Finance Income"

## Balance Sheet Classification
- Non-Interest Banking financing shown separately
- "Financing Receivables — Non-Interest Banking"
- Investment Account Holders' funds classified per CBN guidelines

## Disclosure Requirements
- CBN prudential returns (non-interest specific)
- IFRS-compliant financial statements
- Shariah compliance statement in annual report
- DMO Sukuk disclosure for sovereign issuances
