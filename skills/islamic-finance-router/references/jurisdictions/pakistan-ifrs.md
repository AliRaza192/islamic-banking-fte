# Pakistan — Islamic Banking Jurisdiction Overlay

## Regulator
State Bank of Pakistan (SBP) — Islamic Banking Department

## Accounting Framework
IFRS as adopted by SBP, with Islamic Banking Circulars

## Key Regulations
- SBP Islamic Banking Circular No. 1 ( Shariah Compliance)
- SBP Guidelines for Islamic Banking Institutions
- Companies Ordinance 1984 (financial reporting)
- AAOIFI Shariah Standards (recommended, not mandatory)

## Currency
PKR (Pakistani Rupee)

## Number Formatting
Pakistani format: lakhs, crores (e.g., Rs. 1,50,000 = 1.5 lakh)

## Profit Rate Benchmarks
- KIBOR (Karachi Interbank Offered Rate) — conventional benchmark
- SBP Shariah-compliant benchmark (under development)
- Banks use KIBOR + spread for pricing, but label as "profit rate"

## Zakat Treatment
- Mandatory deduction at source on 1st Ramadan each year
- Applies to savings accounts, PLS accounts
- Rate: 2.5% of account balance (if >= nisab)
- Exemption: Submit CZ-50 form before 1st Sha'ban
- Bank acts as agent (wakeel) for Central Zakat Administration

## Key Differences from Reference Standard
- SBP requires Shariah Board for each Islamic bank
- AAOIFI standards are reference, not mandatory
- Pakistan uses IFRS with Islamic banking modifications
- KIBOR is conventional benchmark (not Shariah-compliant)
- Zakat is mandatory (unlike most Gulf states)

## Income Labels (Pakistan)
- Murabaha: "Profit from Murabaha Financing"
- Ijara: "Rental Income from Ijara"
- Musharakah: "Profit from Musharakah Financing"
- Diminishing Musharakah: "Profit from Diminishing Musharakah"
- NEVER: "Interest Income" or "Finance Income"

## Balance Sheet Classification
- Islamic financing receivables shown separately
- "Loans and Advances" label acceptable with Islamic sub-classification
- Deferred Murabaha Income shown as contra or separate liability

## Disclosure Requirements
- SBP quarterly returns (Islamic banking specific)
- Shariah compliance statement in annual report
- Segment reporting for Islamic vs conventional banking
- AAOIFI-style disclosures recommended but not mandatory
