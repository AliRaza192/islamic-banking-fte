# Indonesia — Islamic Banking Jurisdiction Overlay

## Regulator
Otoritas Jasa Keuangan (OJK) — Financial Services Authority

## Accounting Framework
PSAK 110 (Accounting for Murabaha), PSAK 111 (Accounting for Ijarah), and other PSAK standards aligned with IFRS but with Islamic modifications. Indonesian GAAP (SAK) is IFRS-converged.

## Key Regulations
- Law No. 21/2008 on Islamic Banking
- OJK Regulation on Sharia Banking
- National Shariah Board (DSN-MUI) fatwas
- Bank Indonesia (BI) — now OJK for prudential regulation
- PSAK standards issued by Indonesian Institute of Accountants (IAI)

## Currency
IDR (Indonesian Rupiah)

## Number Formatting
Indonesian format: millions/billions (standard international)

## Major Islamic Banks
1. Bank Syariah Indonesia (BSI) — largest, formed from merger of 3 state banks
2. Bank Muamalat Indonesia — first Islamic bank in Indonesia
3. Bank Mega Syariah
4. Bank BNI Syariah (now part of BSI)
5. Bank Mandiri Syariah (now part of BSI)
6. Bank BRISyariah (now part of BSI)

## Profit Rate Benchmarks
- BNM-RSRR equivalent: Bank Indonesia reference rate
- FASBI (Fatwa of National Shariah Board) on benchmarking
- Banks use BI rate as reference for Islamic financing pricing

## Zakat Treatment
- Voluntary for individuals
- BAZNAS (Badan Amil Zakat Nasional) — national zakat collection agency
- Companies may deduct zakat as expense (tax incentive)
- Zakat Fitrah is obligatory for all Muslims
- Corporate zakat: 2.5% of net wealth (per DSN-MUI fatwa)

## Key Differences from Reference Standard
- PSAK 110/111 are Indonesian-specific (not identical to AAOIFI FAS)
- DSN-MUI fatwa authority is supreme for Shariah matters
- Indonesia has largest Muslim population globally (230M+)
- Islamic banking market share ~10% (growing rapidly)
- Government actively promoting Islamic finance (tax incentives)
- Islamic microfinance (BMT) is significant segment not found in GCC

## Income Labels (Indonesia)
- Murabaha: "Pendapatan Murabahah" (Murabaha Income)
- Ijara: "Pendapatan Ijarah" (Ijarah Income)
- Mudarabah: "Pendapatan Bagi Hasil" (Profit-Sharing Income)
- Musharakah: "Pendapatan Bagi Hasil" (Profit-Sharing Income)
- NEVER: "Pendapatan Bunga" (Interest Income)

## Balance Sheet Classification
- Pembiayaan Syariah (Islamic Financing) shown separately
- Dana Pihak Ketiga Syariah (Third-Party Funds) for deposits
- Pembiayaan Murabahah, Pembiayaan Ijarah, etc. as sub-classifications

## Disclosure Requirements
- OJK quarterly and annual reports
- Shariah compliance statement (per DSN-MUI requirements)
- PSAK-compliant financial statements
- Segment reporting for Islamic vs conventional (if window)
