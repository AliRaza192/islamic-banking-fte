# Saudi Arabia — Islamic Banking Jurisdiction Overlay

## Regulator
Saudi Arabian Monetary Authority (SAMA)

## Accounting Framework
IFRS as adopted by SAMA (full IFRS convergence)

## Key Regulations
- SAMA Banking Control Law
- AAOIFI Shariah Standards (referenced)
- ZATCA Zakat Regulations

## Currency
SAR (Saudi Riyal)

## Number Formatting
Standard international: thousands, millions, billions

## Major Islamic Banks
1. Al Rajhi Bank (largest Islamic bank globally)
2. Bank AlJazira
3. Alinma Bank
4. Saudi National Bank (Islamic banking window)
5. Riyad Bank (Islamic banking window)

## Profit Rate Benchmarks
- SAIBOR (Saudi Interbank Offered Rate)
- Banks use SAIBOR + spread for pricing

## Zakat Treatment
- MANDATORY under ZATCA (Zakat, Tax and Customs Authority)
- Equity-based formula: Share Capital + Retained Earnings + Reserves - Fixed Assets - LT Investments
- Rate: 2.5% per annum
- Zakat REPLACES income tax for Saudi-owned companies
- Foreign-owned portions pay income tax
- Annual ZATCA return filing required

## Key Differences from Reference Standard
- SAMA requires Shariah Committee for each bank
- AAOIFI standards strongly referenced
- Saudi uses full IFRS with Islamic banking guidance
- ZATCA zakat is mandatory (unique among Gulf states)
- All banks in Saudi Arabia effectively operate as Islamic (no conventional retail banking)

## Income Labels (Saudi Arabia)
- Murabaha: "Profit from Murabaha Financing"
- Ijara: "Rental Income from Ijara"
- Musharakah: "Profit from Musharakah Financing"
- Sukuk: "Profit Distribution to Sukuk Holders"
- NEVER: "Interest Income" or "Coupon Payment"

## Balance Sheet Classification
- Islamic financing receivables shown separately
- "Islamic Financing" as separate balance sheet category
- Deferred Murabaha Income netted for amortised cost presentation

## Disclosure Requirements
- SAMA quarterly regulatory returns
- ZATCA zakat return filing reference
- Shariah Committee report in annual report
- Segment reporting for Islamic banking activities
