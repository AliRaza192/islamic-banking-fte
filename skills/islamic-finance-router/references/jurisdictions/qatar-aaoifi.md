# Qatar — Islamic Banking Jurisdiction Overlay

## Regulator
Qatar Financial Centre Regulatory Authority (QFCRA) + Qatar Central Bank (QCB)

## Accounting Framework
AAOIFI Financial Accounting Standards (mandatory for Islamic financial institutions)

## Key Regulations
- QCB Islamic Banking Regulations
- QFC Shariah-Compliant Financial Business Rules
- AAOIFI FAS mandatory (not just reference)
- Qatar Financial Markets Authority (QFMA) — Sukuk regulation

## Currency
QAR (Qatari Riyal)

## Number Formatting
Standard international formatting (millions/billions)

## Major Islamic Banks
1. Qatar International Islamic Bank (QIIB)
2. Qatar Islamic Bank (QIB) — largest Islamic bank in Qatar
3. Masraf Al Rayan
4. Dukhan Bank (formerly Al Khalij Commercial Bank — converted to Islamic)
5. Ahli Bank (Islamic banking window)

## Profit Rate Benchmarks
- QIBOR (Qatar Interbank Offered Rate)
- QCB Repo Rate as reference
- Islamic banks use QIBOR + spread for pricing

## Zakat Treatment
- No formal government zakat collection
- Voluntary individual obligation
- Corporate zakat: typically 2.5% on net assets (per AAOIFI GS 9)
- Banks often calculate and disclose zakat in annual reports

## Key Differences from Reference Standard
- AAOIFI FAS is MANDATORY (same as Bahrain)
- IAH classified as "Equity of Investment Account Holders" (AAOIFI treatment)
- Qatar has large Islamic banking market share (~25%+)
- QFC provides separate regulatory framework for international Islamic finance
- Government actively supports Islamic finance (sovereign Sukuk programme)

## Income Labels (Qatar)
- Murabaha: "Murabaha Income"
- Ijara: "Ijara Income"
- Musharakah: "Musharakah Income"
- Mudarabah: "Mudarabah Income"
- NEVER: "Interest Income" or "Finance Income"

## Balance Sheet Classification
- Per AAOIFI FAS presentation standards
- "Financing Assets" (not "Loans and Advances")
- IAH equity shown separately from shareholders' equity
- Deferred Murabaha Income as contra to Murabaha Receivable

## Disclosure Requirements
- AAOIFI FAS 1 (General Presentation and Disclosure)
- QCB quarterly returns
- Shariah Supervisory Board report in annual report
- Segment reporting for Islamic vs conventional (if window)
- IAH disclosure per AAOIFI requirements
