# Kuwait — Islamic Banking Jurisdiction Overlay

## Regulator
Central Bank of Kuwait (CBK)

## Accounting Framework
IFRS as adopted by CBK

## Key Regulations
- CBK Islamic Banking Regulations
- AAOIFI Shariah Standards (referenced)
- Kuwait Capital Markets Authority regulations

## Currency
KWD (Kuwaiti Dinar)

## Number Formatting
Standard international: thousands, millions, billions

## Major Islamic Banks
1. Kuwait Finance House (KFH) — largest Islamic bank in Kuwait
2. Boubyan Bank
3. Warba Bank
4. Kuwait International Bank (Islamic window)

## Profit Rate Benchmarks
- KIBOR (Kuwait Interbank Offered Rate)
- Banks use KIBOR + spread for pricing

## Zakat Treatment
- Voluntary for Islamic financial institutions
- AAOIFI GS9 calculation method
- Annual disclosure of zakat paid (if any)

## Key Differences from Reference Standard
- CBK requires Shariah Supervisory Board
- AAOIFI standards are strongly referenced
- Kuwait uses IFRS with Islamic banking guidance
- Kuwait Finance House is a global Islamic banking leader
- Dual banking system (conventional + Islamic windows)

## Income Labels (Kuwait)
- Murabaha: "Profit from Murabaha Financing"
- Ijara: "Rental Income from Ijara"
- Musharakah: "Profit from Musharakah Financing"
- Sukuk: "Profit Distribution to Sukuk Holders"
- NEVER: "Interest Income" or "Coupon Payment"

## Balance Sheet Classification
- Islamic financing receivables shown separately
- "Islamic Financing" as separate balance sheet category
- Deferred Murabaha Income netted for amortised cost presentation

## Disclosure Requirements
- CBK quarterly regulatory returns
- Shariah Supervisory Board report in annual report
- Segment reporting for Islamic banking activities
- AAOIFI-style disclosures common
