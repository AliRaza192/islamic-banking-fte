# GCC Cross-Border — Islamic Banking Jurisdiction Overlay

## Regulator
Multiple regulators across GCC states — AAOIFI as common reference framework

## Accounting Framework
Mixed: AAOIFI FAS (Bahrain, Qatar mandatory) + IFRS (UAE, Saudi, Kuwait, Oman)
Cross-border consolidation requires reconciliation between frameworks

## Key Regulations
- AAOIFI Financial Accounting Standards (primary reference)
- IFRS as adopted by each individual GCC state
- GCC Supreme Council resolutions on Islamic finance coordination
- Each national regulator's Islamic banking guidelines

## Currencies
- BHD (Bahraini Dinar) — AAOIFI primary jurisdiction
- QAR (Qatari Riyal) — AAOIFI primary jurisdiction
- AED (UAE Dirham) — IFRS jurisdiction
- SAR (Saudi Riyal) — IFRS jurisdiction
- KWD (Kuwaiti Dinar) — IFRS jurisdiction
- OMR (Omani Rial) — IFRS jurisdiction

## Number Formatting
Standard international formatting (millions/billions)

## Major Cross-Border Islamic Banking Groups
1. Al Baraka Banking Group (Bahrain — operations across GCC)
2. Kuwait Finance House (Kuwait — operations in UAE, Bahrain, Saudi)
3. Dubai Islamic Bank (UAE — regional presence)
4. Abu Dhabi Islamic Bank (UAE — regional presence)
5. Al Rajhi Bank (Saudi — largest Islamic bank globally by assets)

## Profit Rate Benchmarks
- Bahrain: BHIBOR (Bahrain Interbank Offered Rate)
- Qatar: QIBOR
- UAE: EIBOR (Emirates Interbank Offered Rate)
- Saudi: SAIBOR (Saudi Interbank Offered Rate)
- Kuwait: KIBOR (Kuwait Interbank Offered Rate)
- Oman: MIBOR (Muscat Interbank Offered Rate)

## Zakat Treatment
- Bahrain: Voluntary (individual discretion)
- Qatar: Voluntary (individual discretion)
- UAE: Voluntary
- Saudi: Mandatory via ZATCA (equity-based formula)
- Kuwait: No formal zakat obligation (Kuwait Zakat House is voluntary)
- Oman: Voluntary

## Key Cross-Border Considerations
- **Consolidation:** Mixed AAOIFI/IFRS groups need reconciliation adjustments
- **IAH Treatment:** AAOIFI jurisdictions classify IAH as equity; IFRS jurisdictions classify as financial liabilities
- **Sukuk:** Different classification standards across jurisdictions
- **Transfer pricing:** Intra-group Islamic transactions must comply with each jurisdiction's rules
- **Shariah governance:** Each subsidiary may need its own Shariah board, or group board may suffice

## Income Labels (Cross-Border)
Use the jurisdiction-specific labels for each entity. In consolidated financial statements:
- AAOIFI entities: "Murabaha Income", "Ijara Income"
- IFRS entities: "Profit from Murabaha Financing", "Rental Income from Ijara"
- NEVER: "Interest Income" in any entity

## Balance Sheet Classification
- AAOIFI entities: Per AAOIFI FAS presentation standards
- IFRS entities: Per IFRS with Islamic modifications
- Consolidated: Reconcile to parent's reporting framework

## Disclosure Requirements
- AAOIFI FAS 1 (General Presentation) for AAOIFI-primary entities
- IFRS 7/IFRS 16 for IFRS entities
- Cross-border: Additional disclosures for inter-group transactions
- Segment reporting by jurisdiction recommended
