# Oman — Islamic Banking Jurisdiction Overlay

## Regulator
Central Bank of Oman (CBO)

## Accounting Framework
IFRS as adopted by CBO, with CBO Islamic Banking Regulatory Framework. Unique hybrid: AAOIFI Shariah standards are MANDATORY for product structures, but IFRS applies for accounting.

## Key Regulations
- CBO Islamic Banking Regulatory Framework
- Royal Decree No. 69/2012 — Islamic Banking Law
- Capital Market Authority (CMA) — Sukuk regulation
- AAOIFI Shariah Standards mandatory for product compliance
- IFRS mandatory for financial reporting

## Currency
OMR (Omani Rial)

## Number Formatting
Standard international formatting (millions/billions)

## Major Islamic Banks
1. Bank Nizwa — first dedicated Islamic bank
2. Alizz Islamic Bank
3. Meethaq Islamic Banking (Bank Muscat window)
4. Muzn Islamic Banking (National Bank of Oman window)
5. Maisarah Islamic Banking (Dhofar Islamic window)

## Profit Rate Benchmarks
- MIBOR (Muscat Interbank Offered Rate)
- CBO Repo Rate as reference
- Islamic banks use MIBOR + spread, labeled as "profit rate"

## Zakat Treatment
- No formal government zakat collection
- Voluntary individual obligation
- Corporate zakat: discretionary (not mandatory)
- Oman Charitable Organization handles some zakat distribution

## Key Differences from Reference Standard
- Unique hybrid: AAOIFI Shariah mandatory + IFRS accounting mandatory
- CBO requires Shariah Supervisory Board for each Islamic bank
- Islamic banking market share ~15% and growing
- Government actively promoting Islamic finance
- Oman has both dedicated Islamic banks and Islamic windows
- CMA regulates Sukuk issuance separately from CBO

## Income Labels (Oman)
- Murabaha: "Profit from Murabaha Financing"
- Ijara: "Rental Income from Ijara"
- Musharakah: "Profit from Musharakah Financing"
- Mudarabah: "Profit Share from Mudarabah"
- NEVER: "Interest Income" or "Finance Income"

## Balance Sheet Classification
- Islamic financing receivables shown separately
- Per IFRS presentation with Islamic sub-classifications
- AAOIFI Shariah compliance verified separately

## Disclosure Requirements
- CBO quarterly and annual returns (Islamic specific)
- IFRS-compliant financial statements
- Shariah compliance statement (per AAOIFI Shariah standards)
- Sukuk disclosure per CMA requirements
