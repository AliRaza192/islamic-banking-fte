# United Kingdom — Islamic Banking Jurisdiction Overlay

## Regulator
Prudential Regulation Authority (PRA) + Financial Conduct Authority (FCA)

## Accounting Framework
IFRS as adopted by UK (UK-adopted international accounting standards)

## Key Regulations
- Finance Act 2005 — tax equivalence for Islamic finance
- Finance Act 2007 — further Islamic finance tax provisions
- Finance Act 2009 — additional amendments
- PRA Islamic Finance Regulatory Framework
- FCA Conduct of Business rules (applied equally to Islamic banks)
- Bank of England — monetary policy and liquidity

## Currency
GBP (British Pound Sterling)

## Number Formatting
UK format: millions/billions (standard international)

## Major Islamic Banks
1. Al Rayan Bank — largest dedicated Islamic bank in UK
2. Bank of London and The Middle East (BLME)
3. Gatehouse Bank
4. QIB (UK) — Qatar Islamic Bank UK subsidiary
5. Abu Dhabi Islamic Bank (ADIB) UK branch

## Profit Rate Benchmarks
- SONIA (Sterling Overnight Index Average) — conventional benchmark
- Bank of England Base Rate as reference
- Islamic banks use profit rate mechanisms referencing SONIA equivalents

## Zakat Treatment
- No government zakat collection
- Voluntary individual obligation
- UK has active zakat charities: National Zakat Foundation, Islamic Relief
- Corporate zakat: discretionary (not tax-deductible)

## Key Differences from Reference Standard
- **Tax equivalence:** HMRC Finance Acts treat Islamic profit as "interest" for tax purposes
- This is a legal fiction — Islamic profit IS taxed identically to interest
- **SDLT relief:** Stamp Duty Land Tax relief for Diminishing Musharakah (double-transfer)
- **Sovereign Sukuk:** UK issued GBP 200M Sukuk in 2014, GBP 500M in 2021
- **5 Islamic banks** operate in UK (dedicated)
- **Regulatory parity:** Islamic banks follow same PRA/FCA rules as conventional
- No separate Shariah governance requirement — banks use voluntary advisory boards
- Islamic finance market share is small but well-established

## Income Labels (UK)
- Murabaha: "Profit from Murabaha Financing"
- Ijara: "Rental Income from Ijara"
- Musharakah: "Profit from Musharakah Financing"
- Diminishing Musharakah: "Profit from Diminishing Musharakah"
- For tax purposes: These ARE treated as "interest" by HMRC
- NEVER: "Interest Income" in Shariah context (even though HMRC treats it as such)

## Balance Sheet Classification
- Per IFRS presentation (UK-adopted)
- Islamic financing shown as sub-classification of financing assets
- No AAOIFI-specific presentation requirements

## Disclosure Requirements
- UK Companies Act 2006 reporting requirements
- IFRS-compliant financial statements (UK-adopted)
- PRA regulatory returns
- FCA conduct disclosures
- Shariah governance disclosure (voluntary but expected)
- Sovereign Sukuk disclosure per DMO requirements
