# Turkey — Islamic Banking Jurisdiction Overlay

## Regulator
Banking Regulation and Supervision Agency (BDDK) — Bankacılık Düzenleme ve Denetleme Kurumu

## Accounting Framework
TFRS (Turkish Financial Reporting Standards) — IFRS-equivalent issued by KGK (Public Oversight Accounting and Auditing Standards Authority)

## Key Regulations
- Banking Law No. 5411
- BDDK Participation Banking Regulation
- Capital Markets Board (SPK) — Sukuk regulation
- Participation Banks Association of Turkey (TKBB)
- AAOIFI standards referenced but not mandatory

## Currency
TRY (Turkish Lira)

## Number Formatting
Turkish format: millions/billions (standard international)

## Major Participation Banks (Islamic Banks)
1. Kuveyt Türk Participation Bank — largest by assets
2. Albaraka Türk Participation Bank
3. Türkiye Finans Participation Bank
4. Ziraat Participation Bank (state-owned)
5. Vakıf Participation Bank (state-owned)
6. Emlak Participation Bank (state-owned)

## Profit Rate Benchmarks
- TLREF (Turkish Lira Reference Rate) — new benchmark
- TCMB (Central Bank of Turkey) policy rate as reference
- Participation banks use profit-sharing model, reference conventional rates for pricing

## Zakat Treatment
- No formal government zakat collection
- Voluntary individual obligation
- TKBB (Participation Banks Association) provides zakat guidance
- Corporate zakat: discretionary

## Key Differences from Reference Standard
- Turkey uses "Participation Banks" (Katılım Bankaları) instead of "Islamic banks"
- Turkish-language product names: "Murabahaa" (murabaha), "İcara" (ijara), "Müşareke" (musharakah)
- Three state-owned participation banks (government support)
- Islamic finance market share ~7-8% and growing
- BDDK has specific participation banking regulation
- Turkey has active Sukuk (kira sertifikası) market
- No formal Shariah governance requirement (unlike GCC) — banks use advisory boards

## Income Labels (Turkey)
- Murabaha: "Murabaha Kârı" (Murabaha Profit)
- Ijara: "İcara Geliri" (Ijara Income)
- Musharakah: "Müşareke Kârı" (Musharakah Profit)
- Mudarabah: "Mudaraba Kârı" (Mudarabah Profit)
- NEVER: "Faiz Geliri" (Interest Income)

## Balance Sheet Classification
- "Finansal Kiralama Alacakları" (Finance Lease Receivables)
- "Finansman Alacakları" (Financing Receivables)
- "Katılım Hesapları" (Participation Accounts) for deposits
- Per TFRS presentation with participation banking sub-classifications

## Disclosure Requirements
- BDDK quarterly and annual returns
- TFRS-compliant financial statements
- TKBB voluntary Shariah compliance reporting
- SPK disclosure for Sukuk issuances
