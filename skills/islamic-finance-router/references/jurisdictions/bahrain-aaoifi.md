# Bahrain — Islamic Banking Jurisdiction Overlay

## Regulator
Central Bank of Bahrain (CBB)

## Accounting Framework
AAOIFI Financial Accounting Standards (FAS) — primary framework

## Key Regulations
- CBB Rulebook (Islamic Banks Module)
- AAOIFI Shariah Standards (mandatory)
- AAOIFI Financial Accounting Standards (mandatory)

## Currency
BHD (Bahraini Dinar)

## Number Formatting
Standard international: thousands, millions, billions

## Major Islamic Banks
1. Al Baraka Banking Group
2. Ithmaar Bank
3. Bahrain Islamic Bank (BisB)
4. Khaleeji Commercial Bank
5. Kuwait Finance House (Bahrain)

## Profit Rate Benchmarks
- BHIBOR (Bahrain Interbank Offered Rate)
- Banks use BHIBOR + spread for pricing

## Zakat Treatment
- Voluntary for Islamic financial institutions
- AAOIFI GS9 calculation method
- Annual disclosure of zakat paid (if any)

## Key Differences from Reference Standard
- CBB requires Shariah Supervisory Board (SSB)
- AAOIFI FAS are MANDATORY (not just referenced)
- Bahrain is the home of AAOIFI
- All financial reporting must comply with AAOIFI FAS
- "Interest income" NEVER permitted — use AAOIFI labels

## Income Labels (Bahrain — AAOIFI Mandatory)
- Murabaha: "Murabaha Income"
- Ijara: "Ijara Income"
- Musharakah: "Musharakah Income"
- Sukuk: "Profit Distributed to Sukuk Holders"
- NEVER: "Interest Income", "Finance Income", "Coupon Payment"

## Balance Sheet Classification (AAOIFI)
- "Murabaha Receivables" — separate line, NOT under "Loans and Advances"
- "Ijara Assets" — separate line
- "Musharakah Investments" — separate line
- Deferred Murabaha Income — shown as deduction from gross receivables or separately

## Disclosure Requirements (AAOIFI)
- AAOIFI FAS compliant financial statements
- SSB report in annual report
- Shariah compliance statement
- AAOIFI GS9 zakat disclosure
- Movement tables for all major financing categories
