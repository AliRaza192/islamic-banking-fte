## Framework Comparison

| Feature | AAOIFI Primary | IFRS + Islamic Guidance | Local Standards |
|---|---|---|---|
| Accounting standards | AAOIFI FAS | IFRS with modifications | MFRS/PSAK/TFRS |
| IAH classification | Equity | Financial Liability | Financial Liability |
| Ijara treatment | Operating (lessor books) | May derecognize (finance lease) | Per local standard |
| Income recognition | AAOIFI method | EIR (IFRS 9) | Per local standard |
| Zakat guidance | FAS 9 (detailed) | No IFRS equivalent | Per local standard |
| Shariah governance | AAOIFI standards | National regulator | National regulator |
