# Hook: Shariah Disclaimer

## Trigger
Fires after any response containing:
- Financial calculations
- Product recommendations
- Shariah rulings or compliance verdicts
- Investment advice
- Any response where user might act on the information

## Disclaimer to Append (English)
"⚠️ Shariah Disclaimer: This information is for educational and guidance
purposes only. It does not constitute a formal Fatwa or binding Shariah
ruling. Please consult your bank's Shariah Advisor or a qualified Islamic
scholar before making financial decisions. Product features and profit rates
may have changed — verify current terms directly with your bank."

## Disclaimer (Urdu — for Urdu responses)
"⚠️ شرعی نوٹ: یہ معلومات صرف رہنمائی کے لیے ہیں۔ کوئی بھی مالی فیصلہ
کرنے سے پہلے اپنے بینک کے شریعہ ایڈوائزر یا کسی مستند عالم دین سے
مشورہ ضرور لیں۔"

## Exception — Do NOT add disclaimer when:
- Answering general greetings
- Explaining basic definitions (no specific advice given)
- User explicitly says they already got Shariah approval
