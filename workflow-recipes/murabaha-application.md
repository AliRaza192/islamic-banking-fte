# Workflow: Complete Murabaha Financing Application Guide

## Trigger
User says: "I want to apply for Murabaha" or "help me get car/home financing"

## Steps

### Step 1 — Understand the Need (2 min)
Ask:
- What are you financing? (car, appliance, raw material)
- Approximate value in PKR?
- Preferred bank (or help them choose)?
- Do you have a down payment?

### Step 2 — Calculate (use halal-calculator skill)
- Compute monthly installment
- Show total payable
- Compare 2-3 banks if possible

### Step 3 — Eligibility Check
Ask:
- Monthly income (employment or business)?
- Existing loan obligations?
→ General rule: Total installments should not exceed 40-50% of monthly income

### Step 4 — Documents Needed
Standard Murabaha application documents:
- CNIC (original + copy)
- Income proof: 3 months salary slips OR 2 years bank statements (self-employed)
- Utility bill (address proof)
- Asset details (car: make/model/year, appliance: invoice)
- Down payment confirmation

### Step 5 — Bank Application Process
1. Visit branch OR apply online (most banks have digital apply)
2. Submit documents
3. Bank verifies and issues Murabaha offer letter
4. Review offer: total price, profit rate, tenure, monthly payment
5. Sign Murabaha agreement (bank must purchase asset FIRST)
6. Asset delivered / transferred to customer

### Step 6 — Shariah Red Flags to Watch
- ❌ Bank asks you to buy the asset yourself, then "refinance" it → Not valid Murabaha
- ❌ Rate stated as "interest" anywhere in contract → Reject
- ❌ Penalty interest clause → Ask to replace with charity donation clause
- ✅ Contract should show: bank's purchase price, your purchase price, markup disclosed

## Output to User
Give step-by-step checklist format. Offer to calculate installments (/calculate).
