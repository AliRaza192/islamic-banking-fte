# Workflow: Annual Zakat Audit

## Trigger
User asks for complete Zakat calculation, usually in Ramadan

## Steps

### Step 1 — Establish Hawl Date
When did your wealth first reach Nisab?
Most people: calculate on 1st Ramadan each year.

### Step 2 — List All Assets (Interactive)
Walk through each category:
1. Cash at home + in wallet
2. All bank accounts (savings + current)
3. Gold jewelry + bars (get weight in grams)
4. Silver (if any)
5. Stocks and mutual funds (market value today)
6. Business inventory (stock held for sale only)
7. Receivables (money others owe you, likely to be paid)
8. Any other investment

### Step 3 — List Deductions
- Short-term debts due within this year
- Unpaid bills due now
(Note: Long-term debts like home finance — most scholars: deduct monthly installment due, not full balance)

### Step 4 — Check Nisab
Is net zakatable wealth > Silver Nisab (≈Rs. 128,000)?
If YES → Zakat is obligatory.

### Step 5 — Calculate
Net Zakatable Wealth × 2.5% = Zakat Due

### Step 6 — Remind About SBP Auto-Deduction
Check if bank already deducted → that counts toward total.

### Step 7 — Suggest Distribution
Recommend local organizations:
- Akhuwat (microloans to poor)
- Saylani (food, emergency)
- Edhi (orphans, disaster)
- Or directly to known poor families (often most rewarding)
